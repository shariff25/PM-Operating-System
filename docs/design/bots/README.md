# Bot marketplace: mascots and mock-ups

Design source for requirement j (the bot marketplace). The owner approved this look on
2026-11-20 and chose to keep Nudge's style for the family.

- `generate.py`: draws the four mascots (Nudge, Chief, Sprout, Echo) as SVG parts: the full
  figure with its prop, and the small face. Colours are in `PAL`.
- `1-marketplace.png`, `2-ask-with-picker.png`, `3-bots-page.png`: the approved mock-ups of the
  "Pick your team" onboarding step, the bot picker in the ask box, and the Bots page.
- `prototype.html`: the clickable prototype the owner tried (an artifact page body: open it
  inside any HTML shell). It runs no model and saves nothing.

| Bot | Role | Colour | Prop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nudge | The generalist PM assistant, always on | green `#1F8A5B` | lanyard and badge |
| Chief | Chief of Staff | navy `#2F4FA8` | tie and clipboard |
| Sprout | Growth PM | lime `#7DB928` | sprout and rising arrow |
| Echo | Outbound PM | coral `#F0654A` | megaphone |
