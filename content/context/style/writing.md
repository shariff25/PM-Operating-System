# Writing style

- First sentence of a document and of each section is its point: the decision, result or ask.
- Sentences under 25 words; paragraphs of three sentences or fewer.
- Numbers with units, names and dates instead of adjectives ("cuts setup from 2 days to
  2 hours", not "dramatically faster").
- Requirements: one testable statement each, with an ID and P0, P1 or P2.
- Customer-facing text: no unreleased roadmap, no customer names without permission, no
  security claims a security architect has not reviewed.
